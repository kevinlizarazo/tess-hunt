# 0008: The search bounds boxes by the star, skips harmonics of emitted signals, and needs three transits on data

- Date: 2026-10-12
- Status: Accepted (approved by the maintainer)

## Context

The M1 final review's C1: on real stars BLS spent its three signal slots on stellar variability, because the duration
grid was the same 1 to 10 hours at every period and a repeat of the same variable consumed a rank. C2: `min_transits`
only capped the period grid, so a peak with one or two transits on data became a signal. Decision 0007 recorded the
first as a limitation of its injection maps.

## Decision

Search criteria version 2 (`criteria/search-v2.toml`, `[search]`) fills a star's ranks this way:

- **Duration bound** (`duration_factor` 2.0): at each period BLS tries only the durations up to twice the central
  transit duration for the star's mean density (TIC `rho`, else mass over radius cubed, else solar), and always the
  shortest (1 h).
- **Harmonics of emitted signals** (`repeat_harmonics` 1, 1/2, 2, 1/3, 3; `repeat_sigma_p` 2.5): periods within
  `ephemeris_match`'s period tolerance of those harmonics are left out of the argmax and of the SDE's mean and
  standard deviation. Masking a repeat and retrying, the other option the probe measured, spent the retry budget on
  quasi-sinusoidal variables.
- **Transits on data** (`min_transits` 3, `min_points_per_transit` 2): a peak counts only transits with at least two
  binned points. A peak with fewer than three is masked and BLS runs again, at most `max_retries` (3) times; the
  fourth ends the star's search with `budget_exhausted`.
- A non-finite SDE ends the search without emitting the peak.
- TLS leaves the search; 0007 chose `bls30`.
- Every searched star records `n_bins`, `few_transits`, `budget_exhausted`, `n_bls`, `top_period`, `top_q`, `top_sde` and
  `masked_fraction`.

The factor is 2, not 1.5: injections are drawn with the TIC's own density on circular orbits, so they cannot show what
a tighter bound costs when the density is wrong or the orbit eccentric. A factor of 2 allows a density 8 times lower
than the TIC's, or an eccentricity up to 0.6 with the transit at apoastron.

## Limitations

The harmonic exclusion covers the listed harmonics within the match tolerance (1.24%) and nothing else. A
short-period pulsator can still fill later ranks with its other multiples (4x, 5x, 10/3x), and a rotator whose period
drifts with near-repeats just outside the tolerance; a planet on such a star is then missed, as it was under M1's
search. A second planet within 1.24% of a 2:1 or 3:1 period ratio with an emitted signal is left out too. Excluding
more ratios would leave out more planets (3:2 is a common resonance), so short-period variables need their own
treatment, carried to M3. Triage's LEO-Vetter calls these multiples false alarms, so they cost ranks, not false
candidates.
