# 0001: Search slice and endpoint

- Date: 2026-10-08
- Status: Accepted

## Context

Since 2026-08-19 ExoFOP accepts community candidates only after peer-reviewed publication, or in a Research Note of
the AAS whose detection and vetting method was previously published and is cited. A Zenodo preprint does not
qualify. Within a week of TIC 4206066 (2026-10-07), at least five groups posted candidates around nearby K and M
dwarfs.

## Decision

- Search slice A first: dwarfs with 5300 < Teff <= 6500 K, T <= 10.5, at least 4 sectors including one of S97 to
  S107, periods 1 to 40 days. Slice B (long-period duo and tri-transits) follows as a later search mode.
- The endpoint for a surviving candidate is a Zenodo preprint and dataset, transit times preregistered before TESS
  observes again, and a TESS DDT proposal. A community TOI waits for publication.
- Methods stay close to published tools in their published configurations so a later Research Note can cite them.
