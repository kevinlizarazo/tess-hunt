## Title

Preregistered transit predictions for 6 held tess-hunt planet-candidate signals in TESS Sectors 112 to 114

## Resource type

Dataset

## Description

Predictions, fixed before TESS observes them, of where 6 held planet-candidate signals from tess-hunt's search
of public TESS light curves should transit in Sectors 112 to 114, and the test that will judge each
one. Each prediction is the refit that tess-hunt's held-out season check (vet-v3) makes when the season these sectors
make is held out, on the light curve the signal was vetted on, with the training depths that season will be compared
with. The signals are 103312990-1, 241522065-1, 296819076-1, 399023421-1, 423100454-1 and 438857197-1, on TIC 103312990, 241522065, 296819076, 399023421, 423100454 and 438857197. Each is a candidate signal, not a planet.

The package was made by run 20261028T162309Z-predictions-4b7423f-0aa5 at tess-hunt commit 4b7423f3381b1ae4a2f162f23dad342146d1285f, from the vetting records of run 20261020T174317Z-vetting-run-61b5944-2d70. Zenodo
lets a record's files be edited for 30 days after publication; deposited by 2026-11-20, this
package's files lock by 2026-12-20, and no sector starting earlier is predicted. Each signal is judged once, on its
complete new season in a light curve fetched before 2027-12-31; data fetched on or after that date complete no
season, and a season that still lacks a predicted sector in a re-vet judged as of that date or later stays untested.
The README states the test, the data that can test each signal, when it is judged and what a result means.
